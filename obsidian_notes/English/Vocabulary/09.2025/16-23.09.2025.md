- The sudden silence of the forest seemed to foreshow and approaching storm.
- The scientist argued that early symptoms may foreshow the development of a serious illness.
- Ancient rituals were believed to foreshow the fate of entire kingdoms.
- His wet head and hot body was foreshowing that something was going on with his health.
### <span style="color: Blue;">Opposites & Synonyms🌓</span> 
- Synonyms: **foreshadow**, **portend**.
- Antonyms: **conceal**, **mislead**.
### <span style="color: Purple;">3 Famous HighLevel collocation or phrasal verbs</span>
1. **foreshow disaster** – Dark omens were thought to **foreshow disaster**.
2. **clearly foreshow** – Early signs seemed to **clearly foreshow** the company’s collapse.
3. **events foreshown** – The manuscript described **events foreshown** centuries earlier.
### <span style="color: Red;">Other famous forms of the word or affixations</span>
- **foreshowed** (past simple).
- **foreshown** (past participle) – e.g. “the destiny **foreshown** by prophets.”
- Related: **foreshadow** (much more common today, esp. in literature), **omen**, **augury** (formal), **portent** (C1).
