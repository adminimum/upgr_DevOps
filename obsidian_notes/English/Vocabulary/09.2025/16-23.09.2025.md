##  <span style="color: LightBlue;">repose</span>
![Image](repose.jpg)
### <span style="color: Green;">Translate</span>
- _покой, отдых; мир; лежать, покоиться_
### <span style="color: Yellow;">Meanings</span>
1. **A state of rest, tranquility, or calmness**.
2. **To rest or lie down** (often poetic).
3. **To place trust or confidence in someone/something** (formal).
### <span style="color: Orange;">3 Examples with any form</span> 
- After years of restless struggle, she longed for a moment of repose in the countryside.
- The king's body now reposes in the family crypt beneath the cathedral.
- I cannot  repose confidence in a leader who constantly breaks his promises.
- I know that sometimes everyone needs to repose for a moment, even if work doesn't induce to struggle.
### <span style="color: Blue;">Opposites & Synonyms🌓</span> 
- **Synonyms**: **tranquility**, **serenity**
- **Opposites**: **agitation**, **turmoil**
### <span style="color: Purple;">3 Famous HighLevel collocation or phrasal verbs</span>
1. **In repose** – _in a state of rest or calm_
    - Her face, **in repose**, looked peaceful and almost angelic.
2. **Repose confidence/trust in** – _to rely on someone’s character or ability_
    - The citizens **reposed their trust in** the newly elected leader.
3. **Final repose** – _eternal rest, usually after death_
    - The poet found his **final repose** in a small graveyard by the sea.
### <span style="color: Red;">Other famous forms of the word or affixations</span>
- **Reposing** (participle) – _покоющийся, находящийся в покое_
- **Reposed** (adj./verb form) – _покоившийся; положенный с доверием_
- **Reposeful** (adj.) – _успокаивающий, умиротворяющий_
- **Reposefully** (adv.) – _спокойно, умиротворённо_
#repose
